# 👋 Hi, we're TinyIndie

We're a tiny indie studio from Germany making cozy games for your phone, and we shape every update with the people who play them.

## Our games

| Game | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌾 [**Tiny Harvest**](https://tinyindie.de/tinyharvest) | Build your dream farm in a relaxing world of fishing, mining and crafting. Seasonal events and coops with friends. | [Out on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tiny-harvest-cozy-farm/id6755226300) |
| 🏥 [**Tiny Hospital**](https://tinyindie.de/tinyhospital) | Run a cosy little hospital for tiny patients: build rooms, grow herbs, brew medicine and play with friends. | Closed beta |
| 🍋 [**Tiny Lemonade**](https://tinyindie.de/tinylemonade) | Run the coziest lemonade stand in town, from Maple Street to the Starlight Funfair. | [Out on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6821689770) |
| ☕ [**Tiny Coffee**](https://tinyindie.de/tinycoffee) | Inherit Grandma Bea's corner café and grow it into a chain of cozy cafés across the valley. | Coming soon |

## How we work

- **Community first.** Our players shape the games.
- **Cozy, never stressful.** No timers, no fail states.
- **Fair by design.** No ads, no pay-to-win.
- **Radically transparent.** We say what we build, and why.

Read the full version in [our promise](https://tinyindie.de/promise/).
